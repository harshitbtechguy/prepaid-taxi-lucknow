❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Airport-to-Outstation Taxi FAQs

Q: How can I book a taxi from Lucknow Airport?

A: You can book your taxi by filling out the booking form on our website, sending a WhatsApp message with your flight details, or calling our 24/7 helpline.

Q: Can I book a taxi after landing at Lucknow Airport?

A: Yes! Since our fleet is based at Lucknow Airport, you can call us or book via WhatsApp as soon as you land. We will arrange a vehicle within minutes.

Q: Do you provide one-way taxi service from Lucknow Airport?

A: Absolutely. Our core service is providing direct one-way transfers from Lucknow Airport to outstation destinations like Kanpur, Ayodhya, Prayagraj, and Varanasi.

Q: How much does a taxi from Lucknow Airport to Ayodhya cost?

A: The fixed fare for a Sedan (Etios/Dzire) from Lucknow Airport to Ayodhya is ₹3,250. For an SUV (Ertiga) or Innova, the fare will be slightly higher.

Q: How much luggage can I carry?

A: Sedans can accommodate 2-3 standard bags. SUVs like Ertiga and Innova can handle 4-5 bags. Please select the right vehicle based on your luggage.

Q: Can I choose my vehicle? / Can I book an SUV?

A: Yes, you can choose between Sedan (Etios/Dzire), SUV (Ertiga), or Innova/Crysta while making your booking.

Q: Do you provide taxis 24/7?

A: Yes, our airport pickup service is operational 24/7 to align with all domestic and international flight arrivals at Lucknow Airport.

Q: How do I find my driver at Lucknow Airport?

A: Once booked, we share the driver's contact and vehicle details via WhatsApp. The driver will meet you near the designated arrivals pickup point.

Q: Is the fare fixed?

A: Yes, we provide fixed, prepaid fares for outstation routes, so you know exactly how much you'll pay before you travel. No hidden surprises.

Q: Do you provide taxis to cities outside Uttar Pradesh?

A: Yes, we offer intercity transportation from Lucknow Airport to destinations in neighboring states, such as Delhi, Patna, and cities in Uttarakhand.
